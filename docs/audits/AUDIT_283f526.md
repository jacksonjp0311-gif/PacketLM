3. Important scientific weaknesses remain

I inspected the updated acquisition implementation and found four issues that I would prioritize.

Priority	Finding
P0	Undetermined hypotheses with zero support are retained in the exhaustive search path, but excluded from blocking qualification.
P0	Activation pruning can remove empty-image hypotheses that should remain undetermined under open-world semantics.
P1	Iterative deepening stops at the first depth containing one supported procedure, without investigating longer alternatives.
P1	Evidence retirement can preserve a capability’s qualified status even after supporting observations have been removed.

Inspect the updated engine⁠￼

These issues do not invalidate the architectural progress. They do limit what we can claim about the reliability of acquired procedures.

There is also a revealing semantic problem in the geography experiment. PacketLM learned that country means following two containment relationships. Consequently, asking for the country of Texas produces America rather than the USA.

The system learned a fixed relational path, not the full semantic concept of a country.

This suggests our next major reasoning improvement should be type-constrained, variable-depth program acquisition.

Instead of learning:

\operatorname{country}(x)=\operatorname{in}^{2}(x)

Investigate learning:

\operatorname{country}(x)=
\operatorname{firstAncestor}(x,\text{country})

The second procedure could potentially handle different containment depths and identify the appropriate semantic category.

4. The next architectural opportunity

This opens an important research direction: acquiring programs containing conditional operations, rather than exclusively acquiring sequences of relationships.

Eventually, PacketLM should investigate acquiring predicates, branching, bounded iteration, termination conditions and compositional abstractions.

Those mechanisms would substantially expand its reasoning space.
