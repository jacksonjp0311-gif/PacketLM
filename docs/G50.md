# G50: question tie-break (smallest closure first), G49 replication, and `complete` on G46

Pre-registered before the registered runs. It follows G49's registered D1 failure
(docs/G49.md) and the 00:01 steering (America/New_York, 2026-11-27).

## 1. Change: investigate tie-break (programs mode)

**Before.** Among subjects whose answer splits the live hypotheses equally well, investigate
took the first by name. In G49 (seed 537), that picked three sealed depth-≥4 nodes in a row. The
teacher withheld them, and the 6-question budget ran out.

**After.** Ties are broken by the **size of the subject's closure** (entities reachable along
told facts, itself included), then by name.
- *Why it is principled:* a subject with a smaller closure has an answer that depends on fewer
  facts.
- *Why it suits this failure:* shallower nodes in a tree have smaller upward closures.
- *What it does not use:* it says nothing about which items are sealed, and the engine has no
  access to that. It depends only on told facts, so it is independent of storage order and of
  retention. The retention check `prog_question_pool_same_set_under_retention` still passes.
- *Scope:* programs mode, in both the value and the `--answers` investigate branches. The base
  engine is unchanged (L2 stays open, see G49).

**Coverage:**
- checks `g50_tiebreak_smallest_closure_{values,answers}`;
- mutations `g50_tiebreak_off_values`, `g50_tiebreak_off_answers` and
  `g50_closure_size_ignored`.

**Dev** (R arm, seeds 4993–5008; evidence in `docs/evidence/g50/dev`):
- Before the change, seed 5006 round 2 reproduced the D1 failure (0/16 at depth ≥ 4,
  suspended).
- After it, 32/32 T1 tasks are correct.
